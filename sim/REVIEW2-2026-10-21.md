**What the tricks do overall.** On random kits, the targets' standard, even every trick at once keeps every level inside: the Ghost is in about half the parties of 4 and has Spectral in a third of those. The damage is concentrated where a table has a Spectral Ghost: her default Perk, so every first-time table and the one-shot. That is where (a) matters.

**What changes in the one-shot (Hallowby).** Its text states the Spectral florist (1.1 (a) or (b)), the mob formula (1.3), the lock-up's 10 (1.4), the Limit 9 (1.5 E10) and the flight's overdraw rule (1.2 (ii)). Any of those options adopted would need the one-shot synced. Its numbers stay inside the Easy targets under every option measured. The highest is 92.3% with Easy Limit 10; with the trick and option (c), a party with a Spectral Ghost wins 95.2% there.

**How the tricks were played** (upper bounds; REVIEW2 switches in `sim/params.mjs`, off by default):
- *Hand-offs* (`ghostHandOff`): a Ghost with Spectral who holds loot hands it to a friend at the same place at once: after taking it, and on arrival before a pass. "handSteer" also sends her pair to the locations whose last obstacle is a group obstacle.
- *Doomed overdraws* (`doomedOverdraw`): once a chase's rise already reaches the Limit, more Suspicion costs nothing, so the runner overdraws whenever a raise helps.
- *Smart flight pricing* (`flightODPrice`): an overdraw costs 0.25 for each of the overdrawer's own later rolls it actually worsens. So it is free in round 2 for a Soon Entity, or after it has already rolled that round.

**Does our simulator let the Ghost do it today?** Partly. A Spectral pass at a location's last group obstacle already takes the item with no roll (D4), and the partner never rolls. But the simulated players hand loot over only before a watched roll. So after the first such pass she carries it, and Spectral is off for the night: 0.7–0.9 passes a raid for a Spectral Ghost's party, against 1.3–2.0 with the trick. That is why the first review rated Spectral her weakest Perk.

Figures are the 3 seeds pooled (18,000 rolled raids per label) unless a seed range is shown. ✗ = outside the target.

## 1.1 Spectral plus free hand-offs

**Does the simulator let the Ghost do it?** Partly. A Spectral pass at a location's last group obstacle already takes the item with no roll (D4), and the location is done, so her partner never rolls it. But the simulated players hand loot over only before a watched roll, so after the first such pass she carries the item and Spectral is off for the rest of the night. What was missing is the hand-off straight after: the new policy `ghostHandOff` adds it ("hand": she gives her loot to a friend at the same place at once; "handSteer", the generous upper bound: also her group heads for locations whose last obstacle is a group obstacle). The fixes are `spectralFix`: (a) "notLast", (b) "noItem" (she is past, but someone must roll it before it's beaten; alone, she rolls it), (c) "firstOnly".
