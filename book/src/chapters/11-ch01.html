Chapter 1

The Game

You play classic monsters who share a castle in the woods. Once a year, when the town dresses up for its Lantern Night festival, you go down to steal what the castle needs. Get the shopping list home before dawn, and if the job goes wrong, outrun the mob before it corners you with pitchforks.

What a Raid Is

Each raid is on a new town and stands alone. Nothing from earlier raids carries over unless your group uses the optional campaign rules at the end of Chapter 7. A raid runs 1–2 hours, so an evening often fits two. In the story, every raid is the year’s first stock-up.

The Storyteller decides whether the town is Easy, Standard or Hard, using the table in Chapter 8. That choice sets the shopping list’s size, the Suspicion Limit and a few other numbers. It isn’t an obstacle’s Difficulty, the number you roll against.

Who Plays

3–5 players each play one of the eight Entities. One more person is the Storyteller, who runs the town, its villagers and the mob. The tone is horror-comedy for about 12 and up, with real stakes and dark jokes but nothing gory.

What You Need

  • For each player, one each of d4, d6, d8, d10 and d12 (the trait dice), plus a second d6 for the Mask and a second d10 for the Monster, each in a different colour from the trait dice. A 0 on a d10 counts as 10.
  • For the Storyteller, a d20, a d10 and two d6. When the book calls for a d3, roll a d6, halve it and round up.
  • Copies of the Entity Sheet and At the Table for each player, the town’s page and The Eight (Chapter 2), and something to track Suspicion, Turns and the Lead.
  • A town to raid, either ready-made from Chapter 9 or one the Storyteller draws on a map or rolls on Chapter 8’s random tables.

The Raid at a Glance

  • The Storyteller rolls the shopping list. One or two items are essentials and the rest are extras.
  • Visit the town’s locations before dawn (12 Turns), getting past their obstacles and taking the loot.
  • Choose on most rolls whether to hide behind your Mask or let the Monster out.
  • Watch Suspicion, because when it reaches the Limit the whole town hunts you.
  • Get out of town with the goods, or else run for it. Whatever happens, don’t get forked.
