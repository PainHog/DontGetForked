Chapter 5

Suspicion

The whole town shares one Suspicion track, from 0 up to a Limit: 9 on Easy, 10 on Standard and 13 on Hard. A small town notices sooner, so Easy’s is the lowest. With five players, it’s 1 lower on Easy and Standard: 8 and 9.

The Loud Way

When an obstacle lists a loud way and you take it, Suspicion rises by 1, whatever the result, even if an ability made you roll that trait (say, Brute Force where the loud way is Brawn). A trait the obstacle doesn’t list is never loud.

One Roll, One Rise

One roll raises Suspicion only once, by its biggest trigger. The loud way and the Monster showing on one roll make +2 between them, not +3. The one exception is an overdraw, which always adds its +2 on top. A local chase rises only once too, when it ends (Chapter 6). Good results never lower Suspicion.

Tells

The first time anyone reaches a watched location, the Storyteller makes a Tell check: roll a d6, and on 4–6 a Tell goes off. Each place is checked once a raid, for the whole party.

  • The lock-up counts, but not a captive’s arrival.
  • The way out counts once anyone comes back to it.
  • A watched furniture obstacle doesn’t make its location watched.

The check is made once everyone moving there this Turn has arrived. If it goes off, pick one of the arrivals at random with a d6 (two: 1–3 or 4–6; three: 1–2, 3–4 or 5–6; more: number them and reroll unused numbers) and describe its Tell. The only effect is Suspicion +1. Ask first who else is coming this Turn, since a later arrival isn’t checked.

Getting Caught

Trouble at a watched obstacle gets the Entity caught and starts a local chase, which Chapter 6 explains. Only the obstacle you rolled counts. Trouble at an unwatched one starts no chase, even in a watched location, though it still raises Suspicion. Everyone with Trouble in one group check is caught and flees together.

At the Limit

When Suspicion reaches the Limit, or dawn comes with anyone still in town, the hunt is on: every Entity who isn’t captured flees together in the final flight. A roll’s result comes first, so if the roll that reaches the Limit gets the party out, it’s out, and captives it frees join the flight. If a local chase’s rise reaches it, an escaped runner joins the flight; anyone cornered was captured first. Then Suspicion stops, and any extra points are lost.
