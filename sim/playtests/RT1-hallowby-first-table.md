# RT1: the first real table, Lantern Night in Hallowby (2026-10-28)

Richard ran the one-shot for his group on Foundry (release 0.1.13). Reported by Richard on 2026-10-29.

- **Length:** about 2 hours 15 minutes (the script's estimate: 1½–2½ hours).
- **Understanding:** the players understood the rules; they loved the compendiums and charts.
- **Result:** a Win, escaping the final flight by a hair. Suspicion reached 10, so the raid was most likely running on Standard in Foundry (Hallowby's Easy Limit is 9, and the track never passes the Limit): probably started with New Raid instead of the Hallowby macro.
- **Along the way:** no local chases and no captures, so the lock-up and rescues never came up. The players were "gaming the system" (choosing their best traits and Duties) and still only just escaped.
- **Feel:** "a ton of fun", "an absolute blast", "felt like a challenge". They asked for the difficulty levels to differ more.
- **Foundry issues during setup** (the roll window too tall for the Roll button, no box for a Storyteller's raise, the Limit not settable) were fixed the same night (0.1.12, 0.1.13).
- **Follow-up:** Richard's level rules (Standard: Castle Duties locked; Hard: monsters chosen before the list) — measured in sim/LEVELS-2026-10-29.md.
