Chapter 5

Suspicion

The whole town shares one Suspicion track, from 0 up to a Limit: 11 on Easy and Standard, 15 on Hard.

The Loud Way

When an obstacle lists a loud way and you take it, Suspicion rises by 1, whatever the result. If an ability makes you roll the obstacle’s loud trait (say, Brute Force where the loud way is Brawn), it’s still loud. A trait the obstacle doesn’t list is never loud.

One Roll, One Rise

One roll raises Suspicion only once, by its biggest trigger (overdraw included). The loud way and the Monster showing on one roll make +2 between them, not +3. Good results never lower it.

Tells

The first time anyone reaches a watched location, the Storyteller makes a Tell check: roll a d6, and on 4–6 a Tell goes off. Each place is checked once a raid, for the whole party.

  • The lock-up counts, but not a captive’s arrival.
  • The way out counts once anyone comes back to it.
  • A watched furniture obstacle doesn’t make its location watched.

The check is made once everyone moving there this Turn has arrived. If it goes off, pick one of the arrivals at random (number them and roll a die) and describe its Tell. The only effect is Suspicion +1.

Getting Caught

Trouble at a watched obstacle gets the Entity caught and starts a local chase, which Chapter 6 explains. Only the obstacle you rolled counts. Trouble at an unwatched one starts no chase, even in a watched location, though it still raises Suspicion. Everyone with Trouble in one group check is caught and flees together.

At the Limit

When Suspicion reaches the Limit, or dawn comes with anyone still in town, the hunt is on. Every Entity who isn’t captured flees together in the final flight. If the Limit comes during a local chase, that chase ends at once, even if a runner’s own roll brought it there on purpose, and its runners join the flight. Anyone cornered in that same round is captured first, though, and stays behind. From then on Suspicion stops. It never goes past the Limit, and any extra points are lost.
