# Don't Get Forked — brief for the illustrator

*Prepared 2026-10-05 from the decisions Richard Moore has approved (`docs/DESIGN.md`). Anything here that doesn't suit your way of working is open for discussion with Richard: the direction should follow the illustrator, not the other way round.*

## The game in two lines
The players are classic monsters who share a castle in the woods. Once a year, on the town's festival night, when everyone is in costume, they sneak into town to steal what the castle needs — and if it goes wrong, they run from a mob with pitchforks. The tone is **horror-comedy for about 12+**: real stakes and dark jokes, nothing gory.

## Style (approved direction)
- **Storybook gothic woodcut:** bold ink linework and hatching, like an old chapbook or woodcut print.
- **Palette direction:** bone white, ink black, pumpkin orange and moss green, with **festival-lantern glows** as the accent. The exact colours are chosen with you.
- It should read clearly in print and at small sizes (character sheets, the online version's tokens).

## The eight Entities — the heart of the art
Dracula, Frankenstein's creature, the Mummy, the Werewolf, the Invisible Man, a Ghost, a Witch, and Jekyll & Hyde.
- **Each is drawn twice:** in **festival costume** (the Mask: passing as human, almost) and **revealed** (the Monster). That pair is the game's central question on every roll: hide, or be the monster?
- Each needs **one bold visual hook** and a **silhouette nobody could mistake** for another Entity.
- **Jekyll & Hyde** is one character with two forms.
- The Entities' abilities, Weaknesses and Tells (how each gives itself away) are still being written; Richard will share them as they're approved, since they're good material for poses and props.

## Rights — please read
- **Original designs only.** The literary characters are public domain, but the **Universal Pictures film designs are not**: no flat-topped head with neck bolts, no film makeup looks, nothing from the *Creature from the Black Lagoon*, no "Universal Monsters" branding. Name the werewolf "the Werewolf", never "the Wolf Man".
- **Nothing close to *Hotel Transylvania*.**
- Before launch, Richard will have the final roster and designs checked by someone qualified.

## What gets checked (from Richard's first game)
Every piece goes through `book/art/ART-CHECKLIST.md`. The mistakes it was built from:
- **Floating things:** objects that should rest on something but hover (a pencil half off a notepad, a towel with no rack).
- **Anatomy slips:** for example, a cat drawn with one eye.
- **Shapes cut off inside the frame,** such as a half-drawn hook.
- **Impossible angles,** such as a tilted bottle cap.
- **Gadgets that couldn't work.**
- **Sparse scenes.**

The checklist includes an audit of every prop.

## Delivery
- **Vector (SVG) is ideal:** the book pipeline inlines SVG art.
- **High-resolution raster also works:** at least 300 dpi at print size; the pipeline can be adapted.
- Please keep layered source files.
