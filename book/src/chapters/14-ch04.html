Chapter 4

The Raid

The raid happens on the town’s yearly festival, Lantern Night, when everyone dresses up as a monster — which is why real ones can almost pass in the crowd. Dawn ends it.

The Shopping List

Before the raid, the Storyteller rolls the shopping list (Chapter 8; premade raids give their own): 4 items on Easy, 5 on Standard and Hard. One or two are essentials; the rest are extras. Each item is one of six kinds (Chapter 2).

The Town

Every town is built from the same parts:

  • An obstacle: one or two traits that work, and a Difficulty. It is watched or not.
  • A location: 1–3 obstacles, crossed in order, guarding a list item; beat or pass the last and it’s in your hand. Its two ways in are two versions of the first obstacle (say, front door or back window): the party picks one and keeps to it. It’s watched if any obstacle is, either way in.
  • The way out of town, always watched.

Turns

The night lasts 12 Turns. The party starts at the edge of town, by the way out, and gets in without a roll. Each Turn, every Entity makes one roll at its location, moves, or waits (any move takes one Turn unless the map says otherwise). Entities act one at a time, in any order, and each result (a move too) counts at once. Several may try the same obstacle in one Turn; each try risks Trouble. The party may split up. Dawn comes when the 12th Turn ends: anyone still in town runs for it in the final flight (Chapter 6).

Once anyone beats an obstacle (a Success or a Cost), it stays beaten for the whole party, unless it’s marked group (a crowd, a guard who checks everyone): then each Entity who wants past rolls for itself. When several roll it in the same Turn (a group check), all declare their dice and abilities, then roll together; Suspicion rises only once, by the biggest trigger among their rolls.

Furniture

One piece of furniture or decor (they count the same; Chapter 8) stands at one of the list’s locations, behind one extra obstacle, 2 harder (at most 12). Once that location’s loot is in hand, whoever is past all its obstacles may take on the extra one, and the piece. Bringing it home turns a Win into a Grand Year (Chapter 7).

Carrying

  • Bulky pieces need one carrier, Huge pieces two (and won’t fit an entrance a map marks small). Carriers can’t use the Mask, and roll Nimble one size smaller.
  • Carrying, every move takes two Turns (halfway, you’re between places). From the Turn a piece is taken until it leaves town or is lost, Suspicion rises by 1 at the end of each Turn, even set down.
  • Taking a piece is free; drop it any time. Loot has no limit; hand it over free in the same place.

Getting Out

The way out is one watched obstacle: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way. The party leaves together: everyone not captured must be there, and one Entity rolls for all. A Success or a Cost gets everyone out, with nothing more to pay. On Trouble, Suspicion rises, the roller is caught (Chapter 6), and the party may try again next Turn.
