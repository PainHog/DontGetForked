# HW2: Lantern Night in Hallowby, the pre-game check

*Agent playtest and read-aloud check, 2026-10-19, of the current text, two days before Richard runs the one-shot for first-time players. Source at the table: only the one-shot, `book/demo/chapters/*.html` in filename order, its town key (`15-glance.html`), `book/demo/hallowby.json`, the Entity cards (`60-pregens.html`) and the tracker (`70-tracker.html`). The rulebook (`book/src/chapters/*.html`, with `docs/CORE-RULES.md`) was used only for the rules check in section 5. Every random result is a real `node -e` roll (Math.random), numbered R1 onward and listed in the appendix; table choices that could go either way were rolled too. The Storyteller read every box word for word and stopped wherever the text tripped or ran out. Each stop is a finding.*

What stood out:
- **The script still carries a first-time Storyteller through the night.** Every place, result, chase step, capture, escape and epilogue had a page to turn to. None of HW1’s 27 fixes has gone backwards, though one was only half done (the loud-way Trouble lines, M5).
- **One real rules error crept in with the voice pass.** The Captured cue now says only the mirror is gone for the night, so a party whose wine-carrier is caught can read it as leave to steal the wine again. The rulebook says everything the town takes back is gone (H1). It came up in Branch B.
- **One sentence will trip the reader:** “Thorne’s the florist is selling…” sounds like “Thorne is the florist” until the second verb arrives (H2).
- **Most of the rest is placement.** Storyteller instructions inside a read-aloud line at the way out (M1), the “left behind” warning printed after the crossing (M2), the practice roll asking for the second roll before the first results (M3), the Captured box promising a rescue when the final flight starts that same round (M4), the mirror box reading “From now on” when nobody has taken the mirror yet (M6), and a goodnight line that assumes the nightshade came home (M7).
- **Easy is still easy.** Five players, real dice, a Grand Year on Turn 5 at Suspicion 5. Even the worst replay ended in a Bust, not a forking.
