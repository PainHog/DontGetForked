At the Table

The Roll (Chapter 3)

Your trait die (one the obstacle lists; in a chase, the ground) + the Mask d6 or the Monster d10, against the Difficulty (6, 8, 10 or 12).

  • Success: equal to it or more.
  • Cost: 1–2 short. Done, but the Storyteller picks: Suspicion +1, drop an item, lose a Turn, or next die one size smaller.
  • Trouble: 3+ short. You don’t do it; Suspicion +1; caught if the obstacle is watched (a local chase).
  • Critical: a Success on doubles. Two Successes in a chase; anywhere else, a spent charge back.
  • Monster shows: it outrolls your trait die. Suspicion +2.

Abilities (Chapter 3)

One charge each, before the roll, on yours or a friend’s in the same place (not in a local chase), for no action. Make your trait die one size bigger; use the ability’s trait; roll the Monster without risking Suspicion; or open your own approach with an unlisted trait (a Difficulty 2 lower; your own roll, and only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up; never in a chase). One raise per roll. In the final flight, any ability can be a raise. At zero charges, overdraw: Suspicion +2 on top, or in the final flight your Weakness (once a flight, from your next roll, never while it’s in play).

Turns (Chapter 4)

12 Turns, then dawn. Each Turn, each Entity takes one action: roll, move, pick up or wait. A beaten obstacle stays beaten, unless it’s a group one (each who wants past rolls). At a location with your Duty’s kind of item: trait die one size bigger (own rolls; the one raise; not in a chase). Carrying furniture (Bulky: one carrier; Huge: two): moves take one Turn more, no Mask, Nimble one size smaller. Loot: no limit, handed over free. The way out (watched): Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way; one roll for all, once a Turn, everyone not captured there.

Suspicion (Chapter 5)

Trouble +1 · the Monster shows +2 · the loud way +1 · a Cost chosen as Suspicion +1 · overdraw +2 on top · a Tell +1 (4–6 on a d6, the first time anyone reaches each watched place, the lock-up and the way out included, but not a captive’s arrival, and the way out only once anyone comes back) · furniture, each Turn once taken +1. One roll, one rise, by its biggest trigger; one chase, one rise, when it ends. At the Limit, or at dawn, the hunt is on: the final flight.

Chases (Chapter 6)

  • Local chase: Lead 1, escape at 4, against 8 + half the Suspicion as it starts (at most 12); cornered at 0. The Mask allowed (not carrying furniture); abilities on your own roll only; give up any round (captured). Escape: back where you were caught, Turn used. Cornered: captured, and the town takes what you carry. At the lock-up (Difficulty 10, watched): slip free once a Turn from the next (Sly or Nimble, Brawn loud; Success only), or be rescued (Sly, Brawn loud; a Success or Cost frees all).
  • Final flight: everyone not captured flees together; the Mask is off, Suspicion stops, abilities help anyone, any as a raise. Lead 2, escape at 5 (6 on Hard). Cornered: forked.
  • Each round: roll the ground (the chase table); Success Lead +1 (Critical +2), Cost 0, Trouble −1. Several together: more Successes than Trouble, Lead +1 (+2 if ahead by 2 or more); more Trouble, −1 (−2); level, 0.
  • Your Weakness: trait die one size smaller to the end of the chase (Always: round 1; Soon: round 3).

How the Year Went (Chapter 7)

Grand Year: a Win and a piece of furniture or decor. Win: every essential, at most one extra missing. Partial: at least half the list. Bust: less. Forked: cornered in the flight, or everyone held. Each Entity left behind: one step down.
