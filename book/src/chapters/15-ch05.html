Chapter 5

Suspicion

The whole town shares one Suspicion track, from 0 up to a Limit: 12 on Easy, 13 on Standard, 15 on Hard.

What Raises It

TriggerSuspicion
Trouble+1
The Monster shows+2
Taking the loud way+1
A Cost chosen as Suspicion+1
Overdraw+2
A Tell goes off+1

The Loud Way

When an obstacle lists a loud way and you take it, Suspicion rises by 1, whatever the result.

One Roll, One Rise

One roll raises Suspicion only once, by its biggest trigger. Good results never lower it; only a few specific abilities or Perks can, within limits.

Tells

The first time anyone reaches each watched location, check once, however the party has split: if a Tell goes off, the Tell of one Entity arriving there shows, and Suspicion rises by 1.

PLACEHOLDER — how likely a Tell is to go off is set with the Tells themselves, still to be written.

Getting Caught

Trouble in front of witnesses gets the Entity caught, and a local chase starts (Chapter 6). Several Entities caught by one roll flee together. Trouble where nobody is watching still raises Suspicion, but starts no chase.

At the Limit

When Suspicion reaches the Limit — or dawn comes with anyone still in town — the whole town hunts. Every Entity who isn’t captured flees together in the final flight (Chapter 6); a local chase still running ends at once, and those Entities join the flight. From then on Suspicion stops. The track never goes past the Limit; extra points are lost.
