At the Table

The rules on one page. The chapters have the details.

The Roll (Chapter 3)

Your trait die + the Mask d6 or the Monster d10, against the Difficulty: 6 easy, 8 standard, 10 hard, 12 daunting.

  • Success: meet it.
  • Cost: 1–2 short. You do it; the Storyteller picks: Suspicion +1, drop an item, lose a Turn, or your next trait die one size smaller.
  • Trouble: 3+ short. Suspicion +1; caught if watched.
  • Critical: a Success on doubles. A spent charge back, or two Successes in a chase.
  • The Monster shows if it beats your trait die: Suspicion +2.

Abilities (Chapter 3)

One charge each, spent before the roll. Raise your trait die one size; use the ability’s trait; roll the Monster without risking Suspicion; or open your own approach (2 lower, your roll only, never in a chase). One raise per roll. At zero charges, overdraw: Suspicion +2, or your Weakness once the hunt is on (once per flight; from your own next roll).

Turns (Chapter 4)

12 Turns, then dawn. Each Turn, each Entity rolls, moves or waits. A beaten obstacle stays beaten, unless it’s a group one (everyone rolls). At your Duty’s places: trait die one size larger (the one raise). Carrying: moves take two Turns. The way out: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way; one roll for all.

Suspicion (Chapter 5)

Trouble +1 · the Monster shows +2 · the loud way +1 · a Cost chosen as Suspicion +1 · overdraw +2 · a Tell +1 (4–6 on a d6, first visit to a watched place) · furniture, each Turn once taken +1. One roll, one rise, by its biggest trigger. At the Limit, or at dawn, the whole town hunts.

Chases (Chapter 6)

  • Local chase: Lead 1, escape at 4, against 8 + half the Suspicion (at most 12); the Mask allowed, abilities on your own roll only. Cornered: captured (slip free on a Success, or be rescued).
  • Final flight: everyone free; the Mask is off. Lead 2, escape at 5 (6 on Hard). Cornered: forked.
  • Each round: roll the ground (the chase table); Success +1, Trouble −1. Together, the majority rule: 1 toward the side with more, 2 if it leads by 2+.
  • Your Weakness: trait one size smaller (Always: round 1; Soon: round 3).

How the Year Went (Chapter 7)

Grand Year: a Win and a piece of furniture. Win: every essential, at most one extra missing. Partial: at least half the list. Bust: less. Each Entity left behind: one step down.
