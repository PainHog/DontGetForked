Chapter 5

Suspicion

The whole town shares one Suspicion track, from 0 up to a Limit: 11 on Easy and Standard, 15 on Hard.

The Loud Way

When an obstacle lists a loud way and you take it, Suspicion rises by 1, whatever the result. A trait the obstacle lists as loud is loud however you came to roll it.

One Roll, One Rise

One roll raises Suspicion only once, by its biggest trigger (overdraw included). Good results never lower it; only a few specific abilities or Perks can, within limits.

Tells

The first time anyone reaches each watched location (the lock-up included, and the way out when anyone first comes back to it; not a captive being brought in), check once, however the party has split: roll a d6, and on 4–6 a Tell goes off. Roll to see whose, among the Entities arriving: its Tell shows (Chapter 2), and Suspicion rises by 1.

Getting Caught

Trouble at a watched obstacle (it’s the obstacle you roll that counts) gets the Entity caught, and a local chase starts (Chapter 6). Everyone who gets Trouble in one group check is caught together and flees together. Trouble where nobody is watching still raises Suspicion, but starts no chase.

At the Limit

When Suspicion reaches the Limit — or dawn comes with anyone still in town — the whole town hunts. Every Entity who isn’t captured flees together in the final flight (Chapter 6). If the Limit comes during a local chase, that chase ends at once and those Entities join the flight; anyone the same round cornered is captured first and stays behind. From then on Suspicion stops. The track never goes past the Limit; extra points are lost.
