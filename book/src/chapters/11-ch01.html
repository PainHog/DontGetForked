Chapter 1

The Game

You play classic monsters who share a castle in the woods. Once a year, on Lantern Night, the town’s festival, when everyone is in costume, you go down to steal what the castle needs. Get the shopping list home before dawn — and if the job goes wrong, outrun the mob before it corners you with pitchforks.

What a Session Is

Each session is one raid on a town you have never seen before. It is always the year’s first stock-up: nothing from earlier raids carries over unless your group uses the optional campaign rules (Chapter 7). A raid runs 2–3 hours.

Who Plays

3–5 players each play one of the eight Entities. One more person is the Storyteller, who runs the town, its villagers and the mob. The tone is horror-comedy for about 12 and up: real stakes, dark jokes, nothing gory.

What You Need

  • A set of polyhedral dice for each player: d4, d6, d8, d10 and d12; and a d20 for the Storyteller.
  • An Entity sheet for each player (copy the one at the back of the book), and something to track Suspicion, Turns and the Lead.
  • A town to raid: a premade one, a map, or one rolled on the random tables (Chapter 8).

The Raid at a Glance

  • Roll the shopping list: one or two essentials, the rest extras.
  • Visit the town’s locations before dawn, getting past their obstacles and taking the loot.
  • Choose on every roll: hide behind your Mask, or let the Monster out.
  • Watch Suspicion: at the Limit, the whole town hunts you.
  • Get out of town with the goods — or run for it, and don’t get forked.
