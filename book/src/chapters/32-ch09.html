Chapter 9

Three Towns

Three towns ready to raid, one for each difficulty, each with its list, locations, obstacles, furniture, Lantern Night custom, villagers, numbers and map.

A location’s first two rows are its two ways in: the party picks one and keeps to it. The furniture’s obstacle is already 2 harder. On the map, an eye marks a watched location, a star the furniture. Any move takes one Turn.

Easy

Puddlecombe

A sleepy village that has never had a real monster.

Lantern Night: Pies left on doorsteps “for the wanderers”.

The shopping list (4 items):

  1. A wheel of strong cheese (essential)
  2. Seed potatoes
  3. Knitting wool
  4. An almanac with next year’s moons

The furniture: a stuffed bear (Bulky), at the tavern cellar (1).

Villagers: the baker’s wife (looking for a lost cat); the night watchman (tipsy from the cider).

Easy: Suspicion Limit 12 · the way out 6 · the lock-up 10 · the final flight’s mob 10.
