Chapter 7

How the Year Went

When the raid is over, whether the party got out of town or the mob caught it, look at what came home.

An item, or the piece, is home if an Entity who got out of town, by the way out or by escaping the final flight, carries it.

ResultWhen
Grand YearA Win, and the piece of furniture or decor came home too
WinEvery essential home, and no more than one extra missing
PartialNot a Win, but at least half the list home (2 of 4 items, or 3 of 5). A missing essential makes it a Partial at best
BustLess than half the list home (0–1 of 4, or 0–2 of 5)
ForkedCornered in the final flight, or every Entity held at once. The raid is lost, and nothing comes home

Left Behind

Work out the result first, then drop it one step for each Entity left behind in the lock-up. One step turns a Grand Year into a Win, a Win into a Partial and a Partial into a Bust, and two left behind drop it two steps. It never drops below Bust, and a Forked year stays Forked.

The Epilogue

The result decides the epilogue, which is how the castle’s year goes, and nothing else, so nothing carries over to the next raid unless you play the optional campaign rules below. Read the result’s line from the first table below. Then, unless the result is Forked, read a line from the second table for each kind of item that didn’t come home, one line per kind however many items of that kind are missing.

ResultThe year
Grand YearA year of plenty, and the new piece takes pride of place in the great hall.
WinThe pantry is full and the fires stay lit all year.
PartialThe castle gets by, but barely, and the tower stays cold all winter.
BustA long, hard year, with too little to go around.
ForkedThe town tells the story for years, and next Lantern Night it keeps a closer watch.

Silver, china and linenMeals are served on cracked plates, with no clean linen.

Optional: Campaign Play

For groups who play several raids with the same castle:

  • Each piece of furniture or decor brought home, whatever the year’s result, becomes a castle upgrade.
  • At the start of each later raid, the players give each upgrade to one Entity in the party. Each upgrade gives that Entity one extra charge that raid, as part of its starting number, so with one upgrade it starts with 4, and a Critical can refill it up to 4. One Entity may take more than one upgrade.
  • The castle holds three upgrades at most, so a fourth piece replaces one of them, and the players choose which (all upgrades work the same).
  • Nothing else carries over. An Entity forked or left behind is back at the castle for the next raid. Each raid, the players pick Castle Duty, Gift and Perk again after hearing the new list, but the Entities themselves never change.
