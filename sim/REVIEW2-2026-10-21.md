## 1.1 Spectral plus free hand-offs

**Does the simulator let the Ghost do it?** Partly. A Spectral pass at a location's last group obstacle already takes the item with no roll (D4), and the location is done, so her partner never rolls it. But the simulated players hand loot over only before a watched roll, so after the first such pass she carries the item and Spectral is off for the rest of the night. What was missing is the hand-off straight after: the new policy `ghostHandOff` adds it ("hand": she gives her loot to a friend at the same place at once; "handSteer", the generous upper bound: also her group heads for locations whose last obstacle is a group obstacle). The fixes are `spectralFix`: (a) "notLast", (b) "noItem" (she is past, but someone must roll it before it's beaten; alone, she rolls it), (c) "firstOnly".
