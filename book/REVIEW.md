# Don’t Get Forked — rulings and text changes

Every ruling and every change to the rules text gets a row here: what changed, where,
why, and the source (author / playtest / simulator). Until the chapters exist, rows point at `docs/CORE-RULES.md`.

| # | Date | Where | What changed | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Abilities, Suspicion, Chases | Once the hunt is on, Suspicion stops, the Mask is off, and overdraw puts the Entity's Weakness in play for the rest of the flight (no second overdraw) | Overdraw's only cost (+2 Suspicion) meant nothing in the final flight, so every roll there was boosted free (gap G4, S1) | simulator → author |
| 2 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Rolling 4, Suspicion | The Monster showing raises Suspicion by 2 (was 1) | Always rolling the Monster cost under 1 point of win rate, so Mask or Monster wasn't a real choice (S2) | simulator → author |
| 3 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Rolling 3 (P3) | A dropped item falls where you are; picking it up costs that Entity its next action | As a permanent loss, "drop an item" was far harsher than the other Costs (17–22 points of win rate) (S3, gap G16) | simulator → author |
| 4 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES The raid | Getting out of town is one watched obstacle, rolled by one Entity for the party | With the loot in hand the party walked out with no roll, so a raid that went well had no final test (S4, gap G15) | simulator → author |
| 5 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Suspicion, Chases | The final flight's mob Difficulty no longer depends on party size; Tells are checked once per watched location for the whole party | Five-Entity parties were forked in 20% of Hard raids and won 11 points less than three-Entity parties (S5) | simulator → author |
| 6 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Chases (Captured) | A captive slips free only on a Success; a local chase starts at Lead 1; the lock-up is 2 harder | Captures were rare (0.09 per Hard raid) and captives nearly always freed themselves at once, so capture cost little (S6) | simulator → author |
| 7 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES The raid (Furniture, Carrying) | Furniture stands at one of the list's locations behind one extra obstacle; while carrying it, every move takes two Turns | Carrier penalties alone cost almost nothing (carriers let others roll), careful parties rarely had time for a separate trip, and greedy ones lost a Win in a third of raids (S7) | simulator → author |
| 8 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Rolling 3 | A Critical is a Success on doubles: +2 Lead in a chase, otherwise one spent charge back | The rules counted Criticals but gave them no effect; the old Heisty effect (lowering the threat) can't apply here (S8, gap G1) | simulator → author |
| 9 | 2026-10-04 | CORE-RULES Starting numbers | The starting numbers for each label (list size, Difficulty mix, Limit, exit, mob, lock-up) and for every label (charges, Turns, overdraw, Lead) | Tuned in the simulator against the agreed targets under the decided rules (S9) | simulator → author |
