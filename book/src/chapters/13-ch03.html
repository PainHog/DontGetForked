Chapter 3

Rolling the Dice

When the outcome is in doubt, you roll two dice — your trait die and your Mask or Monster die — and add them together.

The Roll

  1. The obstacle sets the trait. Each obstacle lists one or two traits that work, usually one of them the loud way. Using any other trait needs an ability.
  2. Choose the second die: the Mask d6 or the Monster d10.
  3. Roll both, add them, and compare the total with the Difficulty.
DifficultyNumber
Easy6
Standard8
Hard10
Daunting12

Results

  • Success: the total meets the Difficulty. You do it.
  • Cost: 1–2 short. You do it, at a cost the Storyteller picks: Suspicion +1, drop an item, lose a Turn (you skip your next action), or your next roll’s trait die one size smaller. A dropped item falls where you are; picking it up costs that Entity its next action. The Storyteller picks “drop an item” only if someone carries loot.
  • Trouble: 3 or more short. It doesn’t happen, Suspicion rises by 1, and if anyone is watching, you are caught (Chapter 6).
  • Critical: a Success where both dice show the same face. In a chase it moves the Lead 2; anywhere else you get back one spent charge, never above your starting number.

The Monster Shows

If you rolled the Monster die and it came up higher than your trait die, the monster did the work and it showed: Suspicion +2. On a tie, or with the Mask, nothing shows.

The Loud Way

When an obstacle lists a loud way and you take it, Suspicion rises by 1, whatever the result.

Abilities

Using an ability costs one charge. It does one of four things:

  • Raise a die one size (d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12).
  • Use the ability’s trait instead of the one the obstacle calls for.
  • Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it. Trouble or a Cost on the same roll still counts.
  • Open an approach nobody else can take: roll the ability’s trait at 2 lower Difficulty. You are watched as usual.

Spend abilities before you roll. An ability can help any Entity’s roll at the same location; helping costs the charge, not your action. A roll takes at most one raise, from any source, Castle Duty included. Nothing makes a die smaller than a d4: further steps down are lost.

Overdraw

At zero charges you can still use an ability, at a price: Suspicion +2. Once the hunt is on (Chapter 6), overdraw costs your Weakness instead.
