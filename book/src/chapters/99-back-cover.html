Once a year, the monsters go shopping.

Every year on Lantern Night, the whole town dresses up as monsters, and a few real ones walk in with them.

You play Dracula, Frankenstein’s Creature, the Mummy, the Werewolf, the Invisible Man, a Ghost, a Witch or Jekyll & Hyde, who share an old castle in the woods and have a shopping list to fill before dawn. Hide behind the Mask, or let the Monster out and risk the town’s Suspicion. Take what the castle needs, and a piece of furniture too if you’re bold. If it all goes wrong, run, because the mob is coming with pitchforks.

For 3–5 players and a Storyteller · 1–2 hours a raid · ages 12+
