Chapter 2

The Entities

Eight Entities share the castle: Dracula, Frankenstein’s Creature, the Mummy, the Werewolf, the Invisible Man, a Ghost, a Witch, and Jekyll & Hyde. Each player picks one. No two players in a party play the same Entity.

Five Traits

Every Entity has five traits:

TraitFor
BrawnForce, lifting, breaking, fighting
NimbleClimbing, running, slipping free
SlySneaking, hiding, stealing
CharmTalking, bluffing, passing as human
WitsNoticing, knowing, figuring things out

Each trait is rated as a die. Every Entity has one each of d12, d10, d8, d6 and d4, placed differently: what one Entity does with a d12, another fumbles with a d4. Jekyll & Hyde has two forms on one sheet, each with its own arrangement of the same five dice.

The Mask and the Monster

Every roll adds a second die to the trait die. You choose which: the Mask d6, passing as human, or the Monster d10, letting the monster out. The Monster is stronger, but it risks Suspicion (Chapter 3). Every Entity has the same pair.

What Every Entity Has

  • Its dice arrangement.
  • A signature ability, and a second ability chosen as its Gift.
  • A Weakness: something the town can use against it.
  • A Tell: how it gives itself away.

Three Picks

Each Entity is premade, with three picks. Every pick has a marked default, so a new player can skip the choices; to pick at random, roll a d6: 1–2 the first option, 3–4 the second, 5–6 the third (for the Castle Duty, roll on its table, rerolling one already taken).

  • Gift: one of three versions of the Entity’s second ability.
  • Perk: one of three edges, always on and free; each bends one rule in one situation.
  • Castle Duty: one of the castle’s household jobs, shared by all the Entities, each with a small edge tied to the shopping list (hear it before you pick). No two Entities in a party take the same Duty.

Charges

Every Entity has 3 charges. One charge pays for one use of an ability. Charges refill once a year, at the castle; any you don’t spend on a raid are lost, so spend them.

Abilities Never Pass Automatically

A signature or a Gift costs a charge each time you use it and does one of four things: a bigger trait die, another trait, the Monster rolled safely, or an approach of your own (the Draught changes form instead). Each still needs a roll (Chapter 3).

Castle Duties

Every Entity keeps one job at the castle and shops for one kind of item. At a location whose list item is your Duty’s kind, your trait die is one size larger on your own rolls there (the furniture’s obstacle included; not in a chase); it counts as that roll’s one raise (Chapter 3). Each Entity’s entry gives its default Duty; if two defaults clash in a party, the player who chose later picks another or rolls a d6, rerolling any Duty already taken.

The Eight

The entries use terms from Chapters 3–6; a new player can take the defaults and come back to them.

Dracula

Brawn d8 · Nimble d10 · Sly d6 · Charm d12 · Wits d4

Signature: Mesmerise 1 charge

At a watched obstacle that doesn’t list Charm, roll Charm at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He bends a mind with a look.

Gift: Shape of the Night pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Bat (default): Roll Nimble instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). He flits up to the window.
  • Mist: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). If he shows, it’s only fog.
  • Wolf: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). The strength and speed of a wolf.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Hypnotic Eyes (default): On Charm rolls, the Monster shows only if it beats your trait die by 2 or more.
  • Old Money: A Cost on a Charm roll is never Suspicion +1.
  • Wall-Crawler: In a chase you can always roll Nimble, whatever the ground.

Castle Duty (default): Butler, who shops for silver, china and linen.

Weakness: Garlic. Always: from the first round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Every kitchen in town has some, and the mob knows it.

Tell: No Reflection. A shop window shows everyone but him.

Frankenstein’s Creature

Brawn d12 · Nimble d8 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d10

Signature: Brute Force 1 charge

Roll Brawn instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). Stronger than any man, it breaks what others pick.

Gift: Made, Not Born pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Mountain Stride (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He bounds over walls the way he crossed the glaciers.
  • Hovel Watcher: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). He learned to watch unseen.
  • Book-Learned: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). He taught himself from books.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Strong Back (default): You carry a Huge piece alone.
  • Tireless: Carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller.
  • Built to Last: You slip free from the lock-up on a Success or a Cost.

Castle Duty (default): Handyman, who shops for tools and hardware.

Weakness: Fire. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone has to light the torches.

Tell: Head and Shoulders. He stands a head above the whole crowd, and no costume hides it.

The Mummy

Brawn d10 · Nimble d4 · Sly d6 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Signature: Ancient Lore 1 charge

Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). It knew this trick three thousand years ago.

Gift: Secrets of the Tomb pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Royal Bearing (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Charm, roll Charm at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. They still bow to a pharaoh.
  • Just a Costume: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). On festival night, who’d believe a real one?
  • Old Curse: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). A muttered curse, and the lock gives.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Patience of Ages (default): “Lose a Turn” is never your Cost.
  • Keeper of Treasures: “Drop an item” is never your Cost.
  • Fear the Curse: When you flee alone, the mob in your local chase is 1 easier. Nobody wants to get too close.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian, who shops for books and paper.

Weakness: A Loose Thread. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Once someone grabs a loose end, it all starts to unravel.

Tell: Dust and Spice. A trail of dust and a smell of old spices wherever it walks.

The Werewolf

Brawn d10 · Nimble d12 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d8

Signature: Good Dog 1 charge

Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). If the wolf shows, the crowd sees a very large dog.

Gift: The Wolf Within pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Keen Nose (default): Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). It smells the way in.
  • Through the Hedge: At an obstacle that doesn’t list Brawn, roll Brawn at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. It goes straight through.
  • Howl: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). A howl that freezes the crowd for a moment.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Night Runner (default): When you flee alone, your local chase starts at Lead 2.
  • Shortcut: The way out is 2 easier when you roll it.
  • Fetch: The way out is 1 easier, whoever rolls it, while you’re there, and a final flight you’re in starts at Lead 3. You know the way home.

Castle Duty (default): Gardener, who shops for plants and seeds.

Weakness: Hounds. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone lets the hunting dogs out.

Tell: Eyebrows That Meet. Brows that meet in the middle, and a little too much hair everywhere.

The Invisible Man

Brawn d4 · Nimble d8 · Sly d12 · Charm d6 · Wits d10

Signature: Unseen 1 charge

Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). When the Monster shows, there’s nothing to see.

Gift: A Scientist’s Tricks pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Through the Gap (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He slips through as the door swings shut.
  • Poltergeist: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). Things move by themselves, and everyone looks the other way.
  • Work It Out: Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). A scientist’s mind finds another way.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Out of Sight (default): Trouble gets you caught only while you or anyone in the same place carries loot or furniture. They can’t see you, but they can see a floating candlestick.
  • Hidden Pockets: Captured, you keep the loot you carry (not furniture).
  • Light Step: The loud way costs you no Suspicion.

Castle Duty (default): Tailor, who shops for cloth and costumes.

Weakness: Flour. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone throws a bag of flour, and there he is.

Tell: Bandages and Goggles. A wrapped head, dark goggles, a false nose, and a sneeze from nowhere.

A Ghost

Brawn d4 · Nimble d12 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d6

Signature: Through the Wall 1 charge

At an obstacle that doesn’t list Sly, roll Sly at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. Not while you carry loot or furniture: nothing you carry passes through. It drifts in where there’s no door.

Gift: Haunting pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Chill (default): Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). A sudden cold, and fingers fumble.
  • Whisper: Roll Charm instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). A voice in the ear.
  • Fade: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). A trick of the light.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Spectral (default): You get past group obstacles without rolling and without using your action, even in a Turn you move; not while you carry loot or furniture.
  • Rattle: The Monster showing on your roll is Suspicion +1, not +2. Nobody takes rattling chains seriously.
  • Already Dead: Cornered in a local chase, you’re back where you were caught and lose your next Turn instead of being captured (if the Limit comes that round, you just join the final flight).

Castle Duty (default): Butler, who shops for silver, china and linen.

Weakness: Cold Iron. Always: from the first round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Horseshoes, railings, a poker from the fire: every street has some.

Tell: Cold Spot. Candles gutter and breath fogs wherever it drifts.

A Witch

Brawn d6 · Nimble d4 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Signature: Hedge Spell 1 charge

One trait die is one size bigger for one roll in the same place, yours or a friend’s (in a local chase, only yours).

Gift: Witchcraft pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Broomstick (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. In over the rooftops, quiet as an owl.
  • Black Cat: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). Everyone blames the cat.
  • A Potion for That: Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). There’s a potion for everything.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Familiar’s Warning (default): When you arrive (alone or with others), a Tell check goes off only if a second d6 also rolls 4–6. The cat warns you.
  • Fly by Night: In a chase you can always roll Wits, whatever the ground.
  • Wise Woman: A Cost on your Wits roll is never “lose a Turn”.

Castle Duty (default): Cook, who shops for food and drink.

Weakness: Rowan. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. A sprig of rowan, the old charm against witches: somebody’s grandmother always has one.

Tell: A Black Cat. A black cat follows her everywhere and stares at people.

Jekyll & Hyde

Jekyll: Brawn d4 · Nimble d6 · Sly d8 · Charm d12 · Wits d10

Hyde: Brawn d12 · Nimble d10 · Sly d8 · Charm d4 · Wits d6

Signature: The Draught 1 charge

Before one of your rolls, change form: Jekyll to Hyde or back (each has its own dice). The new form lasts until the next draught. Each raid starts with Jekyll. When the Monster shows on one of Jekyll’s rolls, Hyde takes over at once, free.

Gift: The Other Self pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Doctor’s Bag (default): Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10). The right instrument for the job.
  • Pillar of Society: Roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, Suspicion doesn’t rise for it (Trouble or a Cost still counts). A respectable doctor having a funny turn (if the Monster shows, Hyde still takes over).
  • Trample: At an obstacle that doesn’t list Brawn, roll Brawn at 2 lower Difficulty. Only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. A d4 as Jekyll, a d12 as Hyde.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Practised Hand (default): Changing back to Jekyll costs no charge.
  • Steady Nerves: Hyde takes over only if the Monster beats your trait die by 2 or more.
  • Brute Strength: As Hyde, carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian, who shops for books and paper.

Weakness: A Familiar Face. Soon: from the third round of any chase, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone in the mob recognises the good doctor.

Tell: The Wrong Hand. One hand a gentleman’s, the other hairy and knotted.
