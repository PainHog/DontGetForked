# Voice pass: meaning check

Date: 2026-10-13. Compared the rulebook chapters (`book/src/chapters/*.html`) and the one-shot chapters (`book/demo/chapters/*.html`) at commit cfb4d46 with the current tree (HEAD 02cd0fc, nothing uncommitted). Method: HTML stripped, sentences lined up old against new, and a word-level diff of every changed paragraph. Each changed sentence was read against `docs/CORE-RULES.md` (1.33) with one question in mind: would a player or the Storyteller now do anything different?

## Summary

- About 465 old sentences were reworded, merged, split or replaced. 255 are in the rulebook, 65 of them in Chapter 2, where most of the changes are the abilities shortened under the new Four Kinds of Ability box. The other 210 are in the one-shot. No number, die, Difficulty, Lead or Suspicion amount changed anywhere.
- 12 findings: 1 high, 1 medium, 10 low.
- The high one: Chapter 4's definition of a watched location lost a comma. It now reads as if only a watched way in counts, which changes which locations get a Tell check and contradicts Chapter 9's worked example.
- Chapter 2's restructure holds. Read together with the new box, every Entity's signature and Gift versions mean exactly what the old entries said (checked one by one below).

## Checked and found fine (areas of risk)

Chapter 2, each Entity read with the Four Kinds of Ability box:
- Dracula: Mesmerise (“an approach of your own, with Charm, at a watched obstacle only”) plus the box gives the old rule: a watched obstacle without Charm, Difficulty 2 lower, caught on Trouble, only he gets past, never in a chase. Bat, Mist and Wolf are unchanged.
- Frankenstein’s Creature: Brute Force (Brawn instead, the ground’s too in a chase), Mountain Stride (approach with Nimble), Hovel Watcher and Book-Learned are all unchanged.
- The Mummy: Ancient Lore (Wits), Royal Bearing (approach with Charm, any obstacle, watched or not), Just a Costume and Old Curse are unchanged.
- The Werewolf: Good Dog (the box keeps “anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion”), Keen Nose, Through the Hedge (Brawn) and Howl are unchanged.
- The Invisible Man: Unseen, Through the Gap (Nimble), Poltergeist and Work It Out are unchanged.
- A Ghost: Through the Wall keeps Sly and “not while you carry loot or furniture”. Only the reason (“nothing you carry passes through”) is gone. Chill, Whisper and Fade are unchanged.
- A Witch: Hedge Spell is still one raise for this roll, and Using an Entry still lets it go on a friend’s roll in the same place. Broomstick (Nimble), Black Cat and A Potion for That are unchanged.
- Jekyll & Hyde: the Draught is not shortened. Pillar of Society keeps “Hyde still takes over”. Trample keeps its d4 or d12 Brawn note. Doctor’s Bag is unchanged.
- The Four Kinds of Ability box agrees with Chapter 3’s list: a raise for this roll only, the ground’s trait in a chase, what still raises Suspicion, Difficulty 2 lower, Trouble at a watched obstacle, only you get past, the way out and a rescue, never in a chase.
- Using an Entry: same place (a location, the way out or the lock-up), a friend’s roll for your charge but not your action, no help in a local chase, an approach only on your own roll, one raise per roll.
- Castle Duty: your own rolls only, the furniture’s obstacle included, never in a chase, and it counts as the roll’s one raise.
- Weakness and Tell bullets: the mob still brings the Weakness to a chase, and a Tell is still +1.

Rulebook, other chapters:
- Ch 3: the Cost list (drop never the item this roll wins and never furniture; a lost Turn means skipping your next action), Trouble, the Critical in and out of a chase, and a chase roll moving only the Lead.
- Ch 3: the Monster still must be rolled when carrying furniture or in the final flight. Overdraw is +2 Suspicion, or your Weakness in the flight.
- Ch 3: the approach sub-bullets (any obstacle, still watched as marked, others must beat it, way out and lock-up) and helping (even without crossing the obstacles, the roller rolls its own dice).
- Ch 3: netting raises against steps down, then the d4 and d12 limits.
- Ch 4: list sizes and essentials. Two ways in, kept for the raid. The way out and the lock-up always watched. “A place” and “loot” defined as before.
- Ch 4: a move takes one Turn, two when carrying, unless a map says otherwise. Results count at once. Several tries per Turn each risk Trouble. Dawn after the 12th Turn.
- Ch 4: both exceptions to “beaten for the whole party” (group obstacles, an approach of your own), and the group check (declare, roll together, one rise).
- Ch 4: carrying (no Mask, Nimble smaller, two-Turn moves, noise even while set down, abandoning is free and final), dropping (no action, not once caught until the chase ends, stays at the place, between places back at the place you left), picking up as an action.
- Ch 4: getting out (one roll for all, a Cost costs nothing, Trouble catches the roller, captives left behind).
- Ch 5: one roll, one rise (+2, not +3). Tell check timing, the lock-up and way-out exceptions, and +1 as the only effect. Only the obstacle rolled decides a chase. The Limit ends a local chase even if reached on purpose, and anyone cornered that round is captured first.
- Ch 6: Lead starts and escape numbers. The local mob is 8 plus half the Suspicion, rounded down, recalculated each round. Suspicion in a local chase is once per roll, and once a round for a group. Weakness timings (Always and Soon). Nothing dropped or handed over in a local chase. The chase happens inside its Turn.
- Ch 6: capture (the town takes everything, furniture included, gone for the night). The lock-up is a place of its own, one move away, always watched. A captive spends charges on any roll there. A rescue frees only those held at that moment. Left behind by the way out or the flight.
- Ch 6: final flight (Monster only, abilities on anyone’s roll, a drop is left in town), the majority rule and its example, overdraw once per flight and never while the Weakness is in play, and none of it in a local chase.
- Ch 7: one step down per Entity left behind, never below Bust. One epilogue line per missing kind, not after Forked. Upgrades give an extra charge as part of the starting number.
- Ch 8: the obstacle count (the two ways in count as the first obstacle), the ceiling (counted by the d20 before the +2), choosing or rolling the furniture’s item, and villagers changing no traits or Difficulty.
- Ch 8: Costs (never one that costs nothing, nothing to pick at the way out). Slipping free once each Turn after the capture Turn. Furniture costs at least 1 Suspicion, usually 2.
- Ch 9: every die, total, Lead and Suspicion value in the example of play is unchanged (Suspicion 2, 4, 6, 7, 8).
- Reference page and back cover: the Critical line is split but unchanged. The back cover is flavour only.
- Cross-references dropped elsewhere point either to a section in the same chapter (“the chase table below”, “more on that below”) or to something the next chapter covers under a clear heading (Chapter 3’s “drop an item”, covered by Chapter 4’s Dropping and Picking Up).

One-shot:

- The one-shot’s Entity cards and the rulebook’s Entity Sheet page were not touched, and the cards still carry each ability’s full wording, so they don’t need the Chapter 2 box.

## Noticed in passing (same in the old text, not caused by this pass)

- book/demo/chapters/35-places.html, The Mirror: “It adds at least 2 Suspicion before it can leave town.” The rulebook (Chapter 8) and the core rules say at least 1 and usually 2. It is only 2 when the carrier beats the dog itself, because a friend already at the bridge can roll the way out in the Turn the carrier arrives.
- book/demo/chapters/40-happens.html, A Critical: the read-aloud “Nobody heard a thing, and nobody saw” is read for any Critical outside a chase, even on a loud-way roll or when the Monster showed and Suspicion rose.
- book/src/chapters/32-ch09.html, example of play: “(Any ability can go on a friend’s roll in the same place...)” does not cover an approach of your own, which works only on your own roll.
