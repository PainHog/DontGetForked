Chapter 4

The Raid

The raid happens on the town’s yearly festival night, when everyone is in costume — which is why real monsters can almost pass in the crowd. Dawn ends it.

PLACEHOLDER — the festival’s name and customs are still to be written.

The Shopping List

Before the raid, the Storyteller rolls the shopping list on a table (premade raids give their own): 3 items on Easy, 4 on Standard and Hard. One or two are essentials; the rest are extras. Each item points to a kind of location in town.

The Town

Every town is built from the same parts:

  • An obstacle: one or two traits that work, and a Difficulty. It is watched or not.
  • A location: 1–3 obstacles, crossed in order, guarding a list item; beat the last and it’s in your hand. Its two ways in are two versions of the first obstacle (front door or back window, usually with different traits): the party picks one and keeps to it. It is watched if any of its obstacles is, both ways in included.
  • The way out of town, always watched.

Once anyone beats an obstacle (a Success or a Cost), it stays beaten for the whole party, unless it’s marked group (a crowd, a guard who checks everyone): then each Entity who wants past rolls for itself. When several roll it in the same Turn (a group check), all declare their dice and abilities, then roll together; Suspicion rises only once, by the biggest single trigger among their rolls.

Turns

The night lasts 12 Turns. The party starts at the edge of town, by the way out, and gets in without a roll. Each Turn, every Entity either makes one roll at its location or moves (any move takes one Turn unless the map says otherwise). Entities act one at a time, in any order, and each result (a move too) counts at once. Several may try the same obstacle in one Turn; each try risks Trouble. The party may split up. Dawn comes when the 12th Turn ends: anyone still in town runs for it in the final flight (Chapter 6).

Furniture

One piece of furniture or decor (they count the same) stands at one of the list’s locations, behind one extra obstacle. Once that location’s loot is in hand, the party may take on the extra obstacle and the piece. Bringing it home turns a Win into a Grand Year (Chapter 7).

Carrying

  • Bulky pieces need one carrier, Huge pieces two.
  • Carriers can’t use the Mask die, and roll Nimble one size smaller.
  • Huge pieces don’t fit through small entrances.
  • While carrying furniture, every move takes two Turns.
  • Taking a piece is free, with no obstacles to cross again. Drop it any time. Small loot has no limit.

Getting Out

The way out is one watched obstacle: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way. The party leaves together: everyone not captured must be there, and one Entity rolls for all. A Success or a Cost gets everyone out, with nothing more to pay. On Trouble, Suspicion rises, the roller is caught (Chapter 6), and the party may try again next Turn.
