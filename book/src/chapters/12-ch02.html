Chapter 2

The Entities

Eight Entities share the castle: Dracula, Frankenstein’s Creature, the Mummy, the Werewolf, the Invisible Man, a Ghost, a Witch, and Jekyll & Hyde. Each player picks one. No two players in a party play the same Entity.

Five Traits

Every Entity has five traits:

TraitFor
BrawnForce, lifting, breaking, fighting
NimbleClimbing, running, slipping free
SlySneaking, hiding, stealing
CharmTalking, bluffing, passing as human
WitsNoticing, knowing, figuring things out

Each trait is rated as a die. Every Entity has one each of d12, d10, d8, d6 and d4, placed differently: what one Entity does with a d12, another fumbles with a d4. Jekyll & Hyde has two forms on one sheet, each with its own arrangement of the same five dice (he changes form with the Draught).

The Mask and the Monster

Every roll adds a second die to the trait die. You choose which: the Mask d6, passing as human, or the Monster d10, letting the monster out. The Monster is stronger, but if its number is higher than your trait die’s, the Monster shows: Suspicion, the town’s shared alarm, rises by 2 (Chapters 3 and 5). On a tie, or with the Mask, nothing shows. Every Entity has the same pair.

What Every Entity Has

  • A signature ability, and a second ability chosen as its Gift.
  • A Weakness: what the mob brings in a chase to make your trait die one size smaller (Chapter 6).
  • A Tell: how it gives itself away. When it goes off, Suspicion +1 (Chapter 5).

Three Picks

Each Entity is premade, with three picks. Every pick has a marked default, so a new player can skip the choices. To pick at random, roll a d6: 1–2 the first option, 3–4 the second, 5–6 the third.

  • Gift: one of three versions of the Entity’s second ability.
  • Perk: one of three edges, always on and free; each bends one rule in one situation.
  • Castle Duty: one of six household jobs, the same six for every Entity, each with an edge where one kind of list item is found (below). No two Entities in a party take the same one. The Storyteller reads out the shopping list before anyone picks.

Charges

Every Entity has 3 charges. One charge pays for one use of an ability. Every raid starts with all 3 (they refill at the castle, once a year); unspent ones are lost.

Abilities Never Pass Automatically

A signature or a Gift costs a charge each time you use it and does one of four things: a bigger trait die, a different trait, the Monster die without its Suspicion, or an approach of your own (the Draught changes form instead). Each still needs a roll (Chapter 3).

Castle Duties

Every Entity keeps one job at the castle and shops for one kind of item. At a location whose list item is your Duty’s kind, your trait die is one size bigger on your own rolls there, the furniture’s obstacle (Chapter 4) included, but never in a chase. That is the roll’s one raise: no ability can raise it further (Chapter 3). Each Entity’s entry gives its default Duty; if two defaults clash in a party, the player who chose later picks another or rolls a d6, rerolling any Duty already taken.

The Eight

The entries use terms from later chapters: rolls, Success, Cost, Trouble and Difficulty (Chapter 3); obstacles, watched and group, Turns, carrying and the way out (Chapter 4); Suspicion, the loud way, Tells and the Limit (Chapter 5); chases, the ground, the Lead, the lock-up and the final flight (Chapter 6). A new player can take the defaults and come back to them.

Using an Entry

  • Spend before you roll. A signature or a Gift can go on any roll in the same place (a location, the way out or the lock-up): yours, or a friend’s, for your charge but not your action (Chapter 3). Not on a friend’s roll in a local chase, and you can open an approach only on your own roll.
  • One raise per roll, from any source, your Castle Duty’s included. A d12 can’t go higher, and a raise cancels a step down (Chapter 3).
  • Loud stays loud: a trait the obstacle lists as its loud way is loud however you came to roll it (Chapter 5).
  • Perks aren’t abilities: they cost nothing, and one, the Ghost’s Spectral, skips a roll.

Dracula

Brawn d8 · Nimble d10 · Sly d6 · Charm d12 · Wits d4

Signature: Mesmerise 1 charge

At a watched obstacle that doesn’t list Charm, roll Charm at a Difficulty 2 lower; Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He bends a mind with a look.

Gift: Shape of the Night pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Bat (default): Roll Nimble instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). He flits up to the window.
  • Mist: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). If he shows, it’s only fog.
  • Wolf: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). The strength and speed of a wolf.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Hypnotic Eyes (default): On Charm rolls, the Monster shows only if it beats your trait die by 2 or more.
  • Old Money: A Cost on a Charm roll is never Suspicion +1.
  • Wall-Crawler: In a chase you can always roll Nimble, whatever the ground.

Castle Duty (default): Butler, who shops for silver, china and linen.

Weakness: Garlic. Always (from round 1): in any chase, from its first round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Every kitchen in town has some, and the mob knows it.

Tell: No Reflection. A shop window shows everyone but him.

Frankenstein’s Creature

Brawn d12 · Nimble d8 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d10

Signature: Brute Force 1 charge

Roll Brawn instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). Stronger than any man, it breaks what others pick.

Gift: Made, Not Born pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Mountain Stride (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He bounds over walls the way he crossed the glaciers.
  • Hovel Watcher: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). He learned to watch unseen.
  • Book-Learned: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). He taught himself from books.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Strong Back (default): You carry a Huge piece alone.
  • Tireless: Carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller.
  • Built to Last: You slip free from the lock-up on a Success or a Cost.

Castle Duty (default): Handyman, who shops for tools and hardware.

Weakness: Fire. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone has to light the torches.

Tell: Head and Shoulders. He stands a head above the whole crowd, and no costume hides it.

The Mummy

Brawn d10 · Nimble d4 · Sly d6 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Signature: Ancient Lore 1 charge

Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). It knew this trick three thousand years ago.

Gift: Secrets of the Tomb pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Royal Bearing (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Charm, roll Charm at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. They still bow to a pharaoh.
  • Just a Costume: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). On festival night, who’d believe a real one?
  • Old Curse: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). A muttered curse, and the lock gives.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Patience of Ages (default): “Lose a Turn” is never your Cost.
  • Keeper of Treasures: “Drop an item” is never your Cost.
  • Fear the Curse: When you flee alone, the mob’s Difficulty in your local chase is 1 lower. Nobody wants to get too close.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian, who shops for books and paper.

Weakness: A Loose Thread. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Once someone grabs a loose end, it all starts to unravel.

Tell: Dust and Spice. A trail of dust and a smell of old spices wherever it walks.

The Werewolf

Brawn d10 · Nimble d12 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d8

Signature: Good Dog 1 charge

Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). If the wolf shows, the crowd sees a very large dog.

Gift: The Wolf Within pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Keen Nose (default): Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). It smells the way in.
  • Through the Hedge: At an obstacle that doesn’t list Brawn, roll Brawn at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. It goes straight through.
  • Howl: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). A howl that freezes the crowd for a moment.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Night Runner (default): When you flee alone, your local chase starts at Lead 2.
  • Shortcut: The way out’s Difficulty is 2 lower when you roll it.
  • Fetch: Two edges. While you’re at the way out, its Difficulty is 1 lower for whoever rolls it. A final flight you’re in starts at Lead 3, not 2. You know the way home.

Castle Duty (default): Gardener, who shops for plants and seeds.

Weakness: Hounds. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone lets the hunting dogs out.

Tell: Eyebrows That Meet. Brows that meet in the middle, and a little too much hair everywhere.

The Invisible Man

Brawn d4 · Nimble d8 · Sly d12 · Charm d6 · Wits d10

Signature: Unseen 1 charge

Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). When the Monster shows, there’s nothing to see.

Gift: A Scientist’s Tricks pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Through the Gap (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. He slips through as the door swings shut.
  • Poltergeist: Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). Things move by themselves, and everyone looks the other way.
  • Work It Out: Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). A scientist’s mind finds another way.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Out of Sight (default): Trouble at a watched obstacle doesn’t get you caught unless you, or anyone in the same place, carries loot or furniture. It still raises Suspicion. They can’t see you, but they can see a floating candlestick.
  • Hidden Pockets: Captured, you keep the loot you carry (not furniture).
  • Light Step: The loud way costs you no Suspicion.

Castle Duty (default): Tailor, who shops for cloth and costumes.

Weakness: Flour. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone throws a bag of flour, and there he is.

Tell: Bandages and Goggles. A wrapped head, dark goggles, a false nose, and a sneeze from nowhere.

A Ghost

Brawn d4 · Nimble d12 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d6

Signature: Through the Wall 1 charge

At an obstacle that doesn’t list Sly, roll Sly at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. Not while you carry loot or furniture: nothing you carry passes through. It drifts in where there’s no door.

Gift: Haunting pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Chill (default): Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). A sudden cold, and fingers fumble.
  • Whisper: Roll Charm instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). A voice in the ear.
  • Fade: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). A trick of the light.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Spectral (default): Group obstacles (the ones each Entity must get past for itself) don’t stop you: no roll and no action, so you can still move or roll that Turn. Not while you carry loot or furniture.
  • Rattle: The Monster showing on your roll is Suspicion +1, not +2. Nobody takes rattling chains seriously.
  • Already Dead: Cornered in a local chase, you aren’t captured: you’re back where you were caught, still carrying what you had, and you lose your next Turn. If the Limit comes that round, you simply join the final flight.

Castle Duty (default): Butler, who shops for silver, china and linen.

Weakness: Cold Iron. Always (from round 1): in any chase, from its first round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Horseshoes, railings, a poker from the fire: every street has some.

Tell: Cold Spot. Candles gutter and breath fogs wherever it drifts.

A Witch

Brawn d6 · Nimble d4 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Signature: Hedge Spell 1 charge

Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher).

Gift: Witchcraft pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Broomstick (default): At an obstacle that doesn’t list Nimble, roll Nimble at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. In over the rooftops, quiet as an owl.
  • Black Cat: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). Everyone blames the cat.
  • A Potion for That: Roll Wits instead of the trait the obstacle calls for (in a chase, instead of the ground’s). There’s a potion for everything.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Familiar’s Warning (default): When you arrive somewhere that gets a Tell check (Chapter 5), alone or with others, a Tell goes off only if a second d6 also rolls 4–6. That covers everyone arriving with you. The cat warns you.
  • Fly by Night: In a chase you can always roll Wits, whatever the ground.
  • Wise Woman: A Cost on your Wits roll is never “lose a Turn”.

Castle Duty (default): Cook, who shops for food and drink.

Weakness: Rowan. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. A sprig of rowan, the old charm against witches: somebody’s grandmother always has one.

Tell: A Black Cat. A black cat follows her everywhere and stares at people.

Jekyll & Hyde

Jekyll: Brawn d4 · Nimble d6 · Sly d8 · Charm d12 · Wits d10

Hyde: Brawn d12 · Nimble d10 · Sly d8 · Charm d4 · Wits d6

Signature: The Draught 1 charge

Before one of your rolls, change form: Jekyll to Hyde or back (each has its own dice). The new form lasts until the next draught. Each raid starts with Jekyll. When the Monster shows on one of Jekyll’s rolls, that roll stands and Hyde takes over straight after it, free, until a draught changes him back.

Gift: The Other Self pick one · 1 charge a use

  • Doctor’s Bag (default): Your trait die is one size bigger for this roll (a d8 becomes a d10; a d12 can’t go higher). The right instrument for the job.
  • Pillar of Society: Take the Monster d10 as your second die without risking Suspicion: if it shows, that adds nothing. Anything else on the roll still raises Suspicion as usual (Chapter 3). On Jekyll’s rolls, Hyde still takes over if the Monster shows. A respectable doctor having a funny turn.
  • Trample: At an obstacle that doesn’t list Brawn, roll Brawn at a Difficulty 2 lower; if it’s watched, Trouble still gets you caught. Only you get past, but the way out still takes everyone and a rescue frees every captive (Chapter 3). Never in a chase. Your Brawn is a d4 as Jekyll, a d12 as Hyde.

Perk pick one · always on

  • Practised Hand (default): Changing back to Jekyll costs no charge.
  • Steady Nerves: On Jekyll’s rolls, Hyde takes over only if the Monster beats your trait die by 2 or more. Beating it by 1 still shows: Suspicion +2.
  • Brute Strength: As Hyde, carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian, who shops for books and paper.

Weakness: A Familiar Face. Soon (from round 3): in any chase, from its third round to its end, your trait die is one size smaller. Someone in the mob recognises the good doctor.

Tell: The Wrong Hand. One hand a gentleman’s, the other hairy and knotted.
