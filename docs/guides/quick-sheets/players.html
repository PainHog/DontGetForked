Don’t Get Forked · Quick sheet

Player’s Rules

How to play, on one page. The chapter beside each heading has the full rule.

Your Entity Chapter 2

You play a classic monster with five traits: one each of d12, d10, d8, d6 and d4.

Brawn force, breaking · Nimble climbing, running · Sly sneaking, stealing · Charm talking, passing as human · Wits noticing, working things out

Every roll adds a Mask d6 or a Monster d10, in a different colour from the trait dice. A 0 on a d10 is 10. You also have 3 charges, two abilities (a signature and a Gift), a Perk (always on), a Castle Duty, a Weakness and a Tell (when it goes off, Suspicion +1).

The Job Chapter 4

Steal the shopping list (4 items on Easy, 5 on Standard and Hard; one or two are essentials) and get out before dawn, when the 12th Turn ends. A location’s obstacles are passed in order, and whoever gets past the last has the item. Its first obstacle has two ways in: the first roll at either picks it for the raid.

Your Turn Chapter 4

You start at the way out. Each Turn, each Entity takes one action: roll at the next obstacle where it is, move to another place (one Turn; one more carrying furniture), pick up dropped items, or wait. Entities act one at a time, in any order, and everything happens straight away: finish one action, chase and all, before the next. A Success or a Cost beats an obstacle for everyone, except a group obstacle, where each who wants past rolls for itself. Several there in one Turn roll at once, with one rise.

The Roll Chapter 3

Your trait die (one the obstacle lists) + the Mask d6 or the Monster d10 against the Difficulty: 6, 8, 10 or 12. Rolling an obstacle’s loud way is Suspicion +1, whatever the result.

SuccessEqual or more: you do it.
Cost1–2 short: you do it, but the Storyteller picks Suspicion +1, drop an item, lose a Turn, or next trait die one size smaller.
Trouble3+ short: you don’t; Suspicion +1. At a watched obstacle you’re caught.
CriticalA Success on doubles: one spent charge back, or in a chase, two Successes.

The Monster shows when the d10 beats your trait die: Suspicion +2, whatever the result. A tie, or the Mask, shows nothing. Carrying furniture, or in the final flight, you must roll the Monster.

Charges and Abilities Chapters 2–3

One charge a use, and you still roll. An ability makes your trait die one size bigger (a raise), lets you roll its trait instead, rolls the Monster without risking Suspicion, or opens an approach of your own (an unlisted trait, Difficulty 2 lower; only you get past, except at the way out and the lock-up; never in a chase).

  • Spend before you roll, on yours or a friend’s roll in the same place. Helping costs a charge, not your action. An approach, or the Draught, only on your own roll.
  • One raise per roll, from any source; d12 at most, d4 at least.
  • Castle Duty: where the list item is your Duty’s kind, your trait die is one size bigger on your own rolls (not in a chase): the roll’s one raise.
  • Overdraw: at 0 charges, use one anyway: Suspicion +2 on top (only once in a local chase).

Suspicion Chapter 5

One shared track, from 0 to the Limit: 9 on Easy, 10 on Standard, 13 on Hard (five players: 8 on Easy, 9 on Standard).

Trouble +1 · the Monster shows +2 · the loud way +1 · a Cost as Suspicion +1 · overdraw +2 on top · a Tell +1 · furniture, each Turn once taken +1

One roll, one rise: by its biggest trigger (an overdraw on top). At the Limit, the hunt is on: the final flight.

Caught: the Local Chase Chapter 6

Trouble at a watched obstacle, and you run (unless it brings the Limit: then it’s the final flight).

  • The Lead starts at 1. At 4 you escape, back where you were caught, Turn used. At 0 you’re cornered: captured.
  • Each round, the Storyteller rolls the ground: two traits. Pick one, Mask or Monster, and roll against the mob: 8 + half the Suspicion when the chase starts, rounded down, at most 11.
  • Success Lead +1 (Critical +2) · Cost no change · Trouble −1.
  • Abilities on your own roll only, any as a raise; one overdraw a chase.
  • Weakness: your trait die one size smaller from round 1 (Always) or round 3 (Soon) to the end of the chase.
  • 6 rounds at most: then a Lead of 2+ escapes; less is cornered.
  • Give up at the start of a round, once the ground is read: captured, no roll.
  • It raises Suspicion once, when it ends: its biggest trigger (Trouble +1, the Monster +2), plus +2 for an overdraw. Given up before any roll, nothing.
  • Caught together in a group check: one Lead, by the majority rule (below).
  • Nothing can be dropped, abandoned or handed over until it ends.

Captured: the Lock-up Chapter 6

The town takes what you carried, for the night. The lock-up is watched, Difficulty 10. Slip free as your action, once a Turn from the Turn after your capture: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way; only a Success frees you (Trouble: Suspicion +1, no chase). Or a friend there rescues you: Sly, or Brawn the loud way; a Success or a Cost frees every captive, and Trouble gets the rescuer caught. Freed, you act next Turn. Everyone held at once: Forked.

Carrying Chapter 4

Loot: any amount. Hand it over in the same place, or drop it, any time, free (not while caught, never to a captive). Furniture (Bulky: one carrier; Huge: two, who move together; if one is caught it waits, and if either is captured the town takes it): no Mask, Nimble one size smaller, moves one Turn longer, and Suspicion +1 at the end of every Turn once it’s taken, until it leaves town. Set it down free, or abandon it for good (not while caught); taking it back up is an action. Home with a Win, it makes a Grand Year.

Leaving Town Chapter 4

The way out: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way, Difficulty 6 on Easy, 8 on Standard, 10 on Hard. With everyone not captured there, one rolls for all, once a Turn. A Success or a Cost gets everyone out (the Cost costs nothing); Trouble raises Suspicion and catches the roller. Captives stay behind.

The Final Flight Chapter 6

At the Limit, or at dawn with anyone in town, all not captured flee together.

  • Lead 2, escape at 5 (6 on Hard); mob 10 on Easy, 11 on Standard and Hard.
  • The Monster only: the Mask is off. Suspicion has stopped.
  • Majority rule: everyone rolls; count Successes (a Critical is two) against Trouble. Ahead by 1, the Lead moves 1; by 2+, it moves 2; level, it stays.
  • Abilities on anyone’s roll, and any ability can be a raise on any runner’s.
  • Overdraw is a step down: your trait die one size smaller from your next roll after it to the end, on top of any Weakness. Once a flight.
  • Weakness: a new chase, so count its rounds from 1 again.
  • Escape: home with what you carry. Cornered: Forked.

How the Year Went Chapter 7

Grand YearA Win, and the furniture came home too
WinEvery essential home, at most one extra missing
PartialAt least half the list home (2 of 4, 3 of 5)
BustLess than half the list home
ForkedCornered in the final flight, or everyone held

Each Entity left in the lock-up drops it one step (never below Bust).

Tips from the Playtests

  • Pass the essentials around before a risky roll at a watched obstacle: handing over is free, and the town takes a captured Entity’s loot.
  • The Mask never shows. Near the Limit, it’s the safe die.
  • Giving up stops Suspicion snowballing, but costs what you carry.
  • Spend your charges, on friends too. Unspent ones don’t carry over.
