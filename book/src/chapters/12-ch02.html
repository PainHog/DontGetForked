Chapter 2

The Entities

Eight Entities share the castle: Dracula, Frankenstein’s Creature, the Mummy, the Werewolf, the Invisible Man, a Ghost, a Witch, and Jekyll & Hyde. Each player picks one. No two players in a party play the same Entity.

Five Traits

Every Entity has five traits:

TraitFor
BrawnForce, lifting, breaking, fighting
NimbleClimbing, running, slipping free
SlySneaking, hiding, stealing
CharmTalking, bluffing, passing as human
WitsNoticing, knowing, figuring things out

Each trait is rated as a die. Every Entity has one each of d12, d10, d8, d6 and d4, placed differently: what one Entity does with a d12, another fumbles with a d4. Jekyll & Hyde has two forms on one sheet, each with its own arrangement of the same five dice.

The Mask and the Monster

Every roll adds a second die to the trait die. You choose which: the Mask d6, passing as human, or the Monster d10, letting the monster out. The Monster is stronger, but it risks Suspicion (Chapter 3). Every Entity has the same pair.

What Every Entity Has

  • Its dice arrangement.
  • A signature ability, and a second ability chosen as its Gift.
  • A Weakness: something the town can use against it.
  • A Tell: how it gives itself away.

Three Picks

Each Entity is premade, with three picks. Every pick has a marked default, so a new player can skip the choices; to pick at random, roll a d6: 1–2 the first option, 3–4 the second, 5–6 the third (for the Castle Duty, roll on its table, rerolling one already taken).

  • Gift: one of three versions of the Entity’s second ability.
  • Perk: one of three passive edges; each bends one rule in one situation, every time.
  • Castle Duty: one of the castle’s household jobs, shared by all the Entities, each with a small edge tied to the shopping list. No two Entities in a party take the same Duty.

Charges

Every Entity has 3 charges. One charge pays for one use of an ability. Charges refill once a year, at the castle; any you don’t spend on a raid are lost, so spend them.

Abilities Never Pass Automatically

Every ability does one of four things (the Draught changes form instead): raise your trait die one size; use the ability’s trait instead of the one called for; roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion; or open an approach nobody else can take. All of them still need a roll (Chapter 3).

Castle Duties

Every Entity keeps one job at the castle and shops for one kind of item. At a location whose list item is your Duty’s kind, your trait die is one size larger on your own rolls there (the furniture’s obstacle included; not in a chase); it counts as that roll’s one raise (Chapter 3). Each Entity’s entry gives its default Duty; if two defaults clash in a party, the second player picks another or rolls a d6, rerolling any Duty already taken.

The Eight

The entries use terms from Chapters 3–6; a new player can take the defaults and come back to them.

Dracula

Brawn d8 · Nimble d10 · Sly d6 · Charm d12 · Wits d4

Mesmerise (signature): open an approach with Charm at a watched obstacle. He bends a mind with a look.

Gift: Shape of the Night. Bat (default): use Nimble instead of the trait called. He flits up to the window. · Mist: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. If he shows, it’s only fog. · Wolf: raise a trait die one size. The strength and speed of a wolf.

Perk. Hypnotic Eyes (default): on Charm rolls, the Monster shows only if it beats your trait die by 2 or more. · Old Money: a Cost on a Charm roll is never Suspicion +1. · Wall-Crawler: in a chase you can always roll Nimble.

Castle Duty (default): Butler.

Weakness: Garlic (Always). Every kitchen in town has some, and the mob knows it.

Tell: No Reflection. A shop window shows everyone but him.

Frankenstein’s Creature

Brawn d12 · Nimble d8 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d10

Brute Force (signature): use Brawn instead of the trait called. Stronger than any man, it breaks what others pick.

Gift: Made, Not Born. Mountain Stride (default): open an approach with Nimble. He bounds over walls the way he crossed the glaciers. · Hovel Watcher: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. He learned to watch unseen. · Book-Learned: raise a trait die one size. He taught himself from books.

Perk. Strong Back (default): you carry a Huge piece alone. · Tireless: carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller. · Built to Last: you slip free from the lock-up on a Success or a Cost.

Castle Duty (default): Handyman.

Weakness: Fire (Soon). Someone has to light the torches.

Tell: Head and Shoulders. He stands a head above the whole crowd, and no costume hides it.

The Mummy

Brawn d10 · Nimble d4 · Sly d6 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Ancient Lore (signature): use Wits instead of the trait called. It knew this trick three thousand years ago.

Gift: Secrets of the Tomb. Royal Bearing (default): open an approach with Charm. They still bow to a pharaoh. · Just a Costume: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. On festival night, who’d believe a real one? · Old Curse: raise a trait die one size. A muttered curse, and the lock gives.

Perk. Patience of Ages (default): “lose a Turn” is never your Cost. · Keeper of Treasures: “drop an item” is never your Cost. · Fear the Curse: when you flee alone, the mob in your local chase is 1 easier: nobody wants to get too close.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian.

Weakness: A Loose Thread (Soon). Once someone grabs a loose end, it all starts to unravel.

Tell: Dust and Spice. A trail of dust and a smell of old spices wherever it walks.

The Werewolf

Brawn d10 · Nimble d12 · Sly d6 · Charm d4 · Wits d8

Good Dog (signature): roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. If the wolf shows, the crowd sees a very large dog.

Gift: The Wolf Within. Keen Nose (default): use Wits instead of the trait called. It smells the way in. · Through the Hedge: open an approach with Brawn. It goes straight through. · Howl: raise a trait die one size. A howl that freezes the crowd for a moment.

Perk. Night Runner (default): when you flee alone, your local chase starts at Lead 2. · Shortcut: the way out is 2 easier when you roll it. · Fetch: you know the way home. The way out is 1 easier while you’re there, and a final flight you’re in starts at Lead 3.

Castle Duty (default): Gardener.

Weakness: Hounds (Soon). Someone lets the hunting dogs out.

Tell: Eyebrows That Meet. Brows that meet in the middle, and a little too much hair everywhere.

The Invisible Man

Brawn d4 · Nimble d8 · Sly d12 · Charm d6 · Wits d10

Unseen (signature): roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. When the Monster shows, there’s nothing to see.

Gift: A Scientist’s Tricks. Through the Gap (default): open an approach with Nimble. He slips through as the door swings shut. · Poltergeist: raise a trait die one size. Things move by themselves, and everyone looks the other way. · Work It Out: use Wits instead of the trait called. A scientist’s mind finds another way.

Perk. Out of Sight (default): Trouble gets you caught only while you or anyone in the same place carries loot or furniture: they can’t see you, but they can see a floating candlestick. · Hidden Pockets: captured, you keep the loot you carry (not furniture). · Light Step: the loud way costs you no Suspicion.

Castle Duty (default): Tailor.

Weakness: Flour (Soon). Someone throws a bag of flour, and there he is.

Tell: Bandages and Goggles. A wrapped head, dark goggles, a false nose, and a sneeze from nowhere.

A Ghost

Brawn d4 · Nimble d12 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d6

Through the Wall (signature): open an approach with Sly, but not while you carry loot or furniture: nothing you carry passes through. It drifts in where there’s no door.

Gift: Haunting. Chill (default): raise a trait die one size. A sudden cold, and fingers fumble. · Whisper: use Charm instead of the trait called. A voice in the ear. · Fade: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. A trick of the light.

Perk. Spectral (default): you get past group obstacles without rolling, at no action, even in a Turn you move, unless you carry loot or furniture. · Rattle: the Monster showing on your roll is Suspicion +1, not +2: nobody takes rattling chains seriously. · Already Dead: cornered in a local chase, you’re back where you were caught and lose your next Turn instead of being captured (if the Limit comes that round, you just join the final flight).

Castle Duty (default): Butler.

Weakness: Cold Iron (Always). Horseshoes, railings, a poker from the fire: every street has some.

Tell: Cold Spot. Candles gutter and breath fogs wherever it drifts.

A Witch

Brawn d6 · Nimble d4 · Sly d10 · Charm d8 · Wits d12

Hedge Spell (signature): raise a trait die one size, on any roll in the same place, hers or a friend’s (in a local chase, only hers).

Gift: Witchcraft. Broomstick (default): open an approach with Nimble. In over the rooftops, quiet as an owl. · Black Cat: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. Everyone blames the cat. · A Potion for That: use Wits instead of the trait called. There’s a potion for everything.

Perk. Familiar’s Warning (default): when you arrive (alone or with others), a Tell check goes off only if a second d6 also rolls 4–6: the cat warns you. · Fly by Night: in a chase you can always roll Wits. · Wise Woman: a Cost on your Wits roll is never “lose a Turn”.

Castle Duty (default): Cook.

Weakness: Rowan (Soon). A sprig of rowan, the old charm against witches: somebody’s grandmother always has one.

Tell: A Black Cat. A black cat follows her everywhere and stares at people.

Jekyll & Hyde

Jekyll: Brawn d4 · Nimble d6 · Sly d8 · Charm d12 · Wits d10

Hyde: Brawn d12 · Nimble d10 · Sly d8 · Charm d4 · Wits d6

The Draught (signature): change form, Jekyll to Hyde or back, before one of your rolls; the new form lasts until the next draught. He starts each raid as Jekyll. When the Monster shows on one of Jekyll’s rolls, Hyde takes over, free.

Gift: The Other Self. Doctor’s Bag (default): raise a trait die one size. The right instrument for the job. · Pillar of Society: roll the Monster die without risking Suspicion. A respectable doctor having a funny turn (if the Monster shows, Hyde still takes over). · Trample: open an approach with Brawn. A d4 as Jekyll, a d12 as Hyde.

Perk. Practised Hand (default): changing back to Jekyll costs no charge. · Steady Nerves: Hyde takes over only if the Monster beats your trait die by 2 or more. · Brute Strength: as Hyde, carrying doesn’t make your Nimble smaller.

Castle Duty (default): Librarian.

Weakness: A Familiar Face (Soon). Someone in the mob recognises the good doctor.

Tell: The Wrong Hand. One hand a gentleman’s, the other hairy and knotted.
