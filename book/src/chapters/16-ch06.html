Chapter 6

Chases and Getting Forked

There are two kinds of chase. Get caught during the raid and you run from a crowd; botch the whole job and the entire town turns out with pitchforks.

The Lead

Every chase uses a Lead track. Each round you roll against the mob’s Difficulty:

  • Success: Lead +1 (a Critical: +2).
  • Cost: no change.
  • Trouble: Lead −1.

Reach the escape number and you are clear. Reach 0 and you are cornered.

The Ground

Each round, roll a d6 on the chase table at the end of this chapter. The result says which traits work this round, and always includes one that isn’t Nimble.

Your Weakness

Once the mob brings your Weakness, you roll your trait one size smaller for the rest of the chase.

PLACEHOLDER — when the mob brings each Weakness is set with the Weaknesses themselves, still to be written.

The Local Chase

When you are caught during the raid, you run. Your Lead starts at 1 and you escape at 4. The mob’s Difficulty is 10 plus half the Suspicion (round down), at most 12. Rolls in a local chase raise Suspicion as usual, and the chase happens within the Turn it started. Several Entities caught by one roll flee together on one shared Lead, which moves by the majority rule below, and are captured together if cornered.

Captured

Cornered in a local chase, you are captured, and the town takes back what you were carrying. You are held at the town’s lock-up.

  • Rescue: the lock-up is an obstacle — Sly, or Brawn the loud way, always watched — at the lock-up Difficulty, with a new rescue obstacle for each capture. Getting there costs the party Turns.
  • Slipping free: once per Turn, a captive may try to slip free. Only a Success frees you; on Trouble, Suspicion rises.
  • Left behind: anyone still held when the party leaves town is left behind (Chapter 7).

The Final Flight

At the Limit, or at dawn, every Entity who isn’t captured flees together. The Lead starts at 2 and the party escapes at 6. The mob’s Difficulty is set by the town’s difficulty (Chapter 8) and doesn’t change with the size of the party.

The Majority Rule

Everyone rolls each round. If Successes outnumber Trouble, the Lead rises by 1; if Trouble outnumbers Successes, it falls by 1; otherwise it stays. A Critical counts as two Successes.

Escape and you are home with the goods. Get cornered and the party is forked: the monsters are killed and the raid is lost.

Once the Hunt Is On

From the moment the final flight begins, Suspicion stops and the Mask is off: everyone rolls the Monster die. You may still overdraw, but your Weakness is then in play for the rest of the flight, and once your Weakness is in play you can’t overdraw again.
