# Don't Get Forked v0.1: review of layout, art and image rights

**What I reviewed:** the 34-page US Letter PDF (`dgf.pdf`, built 14 Oct 2026). I rendered every page at 90 dpi and viewed them as two-page spreads. I zoomed doubtful areas to 150–300 dpi and pulled all 35 embedded images out at full resolution.

**How I measured:** effective ppi is the image's pixel size divided by its placed size. The "upscaled" calls come from a resample test: shrinking the image and enlarging it again loses almost nothing when the scan held little real detail.

**Repositories:** none were changed. This is not legal advice; rights notes are risk flags only.

Severity: **must-fix** / **should-fix** / **nice-to-have**.

---

## Top issues (in priority order)

1. **Four pages are near-empty or padded with filler pictures** (pp. 15, 19, 21, 27), and p. 13 is half filler. Chapters always start on a new page, so each chapter ends with spare space. The spare space is either left blank or filled with a full-width stand-in. (must-fix)
2. **Two portrait stand-ins could read as offensive caricature at print size:**
   - p. 10, the Invisible Man "In costume": two men with long hooked false noses whispering in top hats.
   - p. 8, Frankenstein "In costume": a procession giant with a blacked-out face.

   (should-fix; swap before anyone outside the table sees it)
3. **Rights:** I found no film stills, publicity photos or photographs of any kind. Every image looks like a pre-1931 print or drawing. The open rights items are:
   - [Storefront-disclosure note left out of the repository at Richard's request; he has the original.]
   - no **per-image source log**;
   - Wikimedia files and a few 20th-century artists (Strimpl, Koster, Woodward) need their dates and sources checked.
4. **Weak or wrong-reading stand-ins:**
   - Dracula "Revealed" is a plumed cavalier from a theatre costume plate.
   - Invisible Man "Revealed" is a skeleton.
   - The Chapter 1 castle banner is a smeary upscale.
   - Three full-width pictures sit at about 126 ppi (pp. 19, 21, 27).
5. **Smaller layout faults:**
   - Frankenstein's entry splits across pp. 8–9.
   - The key that explains the town pages comes after the first town.
   - A table cell wraps into the wrong column (p. 28).
   - Shopping-list numbers wrap away from their items (p. 25).
   - Footers are inconsistent.
   - The back cover says "2–3 hours"; the rules say 1–2.
