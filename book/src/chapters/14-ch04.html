Chapter 4

The Raid

The raid happens on the town’s yearly festival, Lantern Night, when everyone dresses up as a monster — which is why real ones can almost pass in the crowd. Dawn ends it.

The Shopping List

Before the raid, the Storyteller rolls the shopping list (Chapter 8; Chapter 9’s towns have theirs): 4 items on Easy, 5 on Standard and Hard. One or two are essentials; the rest are extras. Each item is one of six kinds (Chapter 2).

The Town

  • An obstacle: one or two traits that work, and a Difficulty. It is watched or not.
  • A location: 1–3 obstacles, crossed in order, guarding a list item (or two sharing a place); beat or pass the last and the loot is in your hand. Its two ways in are two versions of the first obstacle (say, front door or back window): the party picks one and keeps to it. It’s watched if any obstacle is, either way in.
  • The way out of town, always watched.

Turns

The night lasts 12 Turns. The party starts at the edge of town, by the way out, and gets in without a roll. Each Turn, every Entity takes one action: a roll at its location, a move, or waiting (any move takes one Turn unless the map says otherwise). Entities act one at a time, in any order, and each result (a move too) counts at once. Several may try the same obstacle in one Turn; each try risks Trouble. The party may split up, and on Standard and Hard it usually should: there’s rarely time to visit every place together. Dawn comes when the 12th Turn ends: anyone still in town runs for it in the final flight (Chapter 6).

Once anyone beats an obstacle (a Success or a Cost), it stays beaten for the whole party, unless it’s marked group (a crowd, a guard who checks everyone): then each Entity who wants past rolls for itself. When several roll it in the same Turn (a group check), all declare their dice and abilities, then roll together; Suspicion rises only once, by the biggest trigger among their rolls.

Furniture

One piece of furniture or decor (Chapter 8) stands at one of the list’s locations, behind one extra obstacle, 2 harder (at most 12). Once that location’s loot is in hand, whoever is past all its obstacles may try for the piece. Bringing it home turns a Win into a Grand Year (Chapter 7).

Carrying

  • Bulky pieces need one carrier, Huge two (not through an entrance a map marks small). Carriers can’t use the Mask; their Nimble is one size smaller.
  • Carrying, a move takes two Turns: you’re between places until you act in the second.
  • Taking a piece is free (taking it back up is an action); drop it any time but in a local chase. Once taken, it raises Suspicion by 1 at the end of each Turn, even set down, until it’s carried out of town, lost or abandoned (free: it stays put, goes quiet, can’t be taken again).
  • Loot has no limit; hand it over free in the same place.

Getting Out

The way out is one watched obstacle: Sly or Nimble, or Brawn the loud way. The party leaves together: everyone not captured is there, and one rolls for all. A Success or a Cost gets everyone out, free. On Trouble, Suspicion rises and the roller is caught (Chapter 6); try again next Turn.
