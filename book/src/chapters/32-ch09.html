Chapter 9

Three Towns

Three towns ready to raid, one for each difficulty, each with its list, locations, obstacles, furniture, Lantern Night custom, villagers (its faces), numbers and map.

A location’s first two rows are its two ways in: the party picks one and keeps to it. The furniture’s obstacle is already 2 harder. On the map, an eye marks a watched location, a star the furniture. Any move takes one Turn.

An Example of Play

Four players raid Thistlewick: Dracula, the Witch, Frankenstein’s Creature and the Werewolf, with their default picks (Butler, Cook, Handyman and Gardener). Suspicion starts at 0; the Limit is 11.

Turn 1. The party splits. The Witch and the Werewolf head for the baker, where the Witch’s Cook Duty gives her an edge; Dracula and the Creature take the china shop, Dracula’s as Butler. Both are watched, so the Storyteller makes a Tell check at each: a 2 and a 1, no Tells.

Turn 2. At the baker, the party takes the shuttered window. The Werewolf rolls Nimble d12 with the Mask: 10 + 4 = 14 against 8, a Success. The window is open for both of them now, so the Witch goes on to the shopkeeper (Charm, 8, watched). Her Charm d8 is a d10 here (her Duty’s raise), and she lets the Monster out for the bigger die: 1 + 4 = 5. That is 3 short, Trouble, and the Monster’s 4 beat her trait die’s 1, so it showed. One roll, one rise: Suspicion goes up by the bigger trigger, to 2. The shopkeeper was watching, so the Witch is caught.

She runs. Her Lead starts at 1, and the mob is 8 + half the Suspicion: 9. Round 1, a dead end: Wits d12 and the Monster, 12 + 3 = 15, Lead 2. Round 2, another dead end: 2 + 9 = 11, Lead 3, but the Monster showed: Suspicion 4, and the mob is now 10. Round 3, the festival parade, and out comes a sprig of rowan: her Weakness makes her Sly d10 a d8. She spends a charge on her Hedge Spell to raise it back and rolls 5 + 7 = 12: Lead 4, and she escapes. The Monster showed again: Suspicion 6. She is back at the baker, her Turn used up.

At the china shop, Dracula picks the lock of the front door with the Mask: Sly d6, a d8 with his Butler Duty, 7 + 2 = 9 against 8. The Creature takes the back room with Wits d10 and the Mask: 5 + 2 = 7, one short, a Cost. He has the tea service; the Storyteller picks the Cost. Dropping an item would cost nothing (he carries only what this roll won), so it’s Suspicion +1: a teacup smashes. Suspicion 7.

Turn 3. The Witch tries the shopkeeper again, and the Werewolf spends a charge on Good Dog for her: she rolls the Monster without risking Suspicion. 7 + 4 = 11, a Success, and the wedding cake is hers. Dracula and the Creature move on to the ironmonger, which is watched: the Tell check is a 4, and a second roll picks the Creature, who stands a head above the crowd. Suspicion 8.

Three Turns gone, nine to go. Both essentials are in hand, but Suspicion is 8 of 11: from here on, every Monster counts.
