Foreword

From the Designer

Where did the idea come from?

I was trying to think of a Halloween tabletop game. My table runs something every year. We always aim for a one-shot, but it sometimes takes us a few weeks anyways. I came up with the idea of monsters having to restock their supplies, and the name just came to me. Pitchforks, and it sounded funny.

Why classic monsters, and why these eight?

They are easily referenced historical figures that gave diversity to the game. I didn’t want to fabricate a ton of characters for this. I actually favor Jekyll & Hyde.

Why a shopping trip?

I mean, monsters needing supplies, and that’s how the villagers catch them. I thought it just fit the idea of being chased away, like robbers get chased. But what makes it funny is being monsters, trying not to be seen as monsters, stealing things for your monster castle.

Why the Mask or the Monster on every roll?

Monsters generally don’t want to be found out as monsters. That’s how villages chase you down and kill you, or burn your castle down. Sometimes you need to be the monster, though. That’s the point of being a monster! But they are living in a human world.

What should a group expect from its first night?

Chaos, mishaps, running with loot, excitement.

Richard Moore
